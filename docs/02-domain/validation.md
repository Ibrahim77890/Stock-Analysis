# Validation tools (Sprint 04)

Code in `services/market-data/src/market_data/`: `validation.py` (grid search, registry, splits, holdout, walk-forward), `sharpe_stats.py` (PSR, DSR, overfitting meter), `scan.py` (universe scan, best-of-N control), `regimes.py` (regime breakdown), `api_validation.py` (Swagger section "Validation"). Protocol: [ADR 0012](../04-adr/0012-validation-protocol.md). Real numbers: [validation-worked-example.md](validation-worked-example.md) (generated by `scripts/validation_worked_example.py`). Data window: see "Bars per symbol (Sprint 04 audit)" in data-profile-v1.md: **the contiguous window of the whole universe is 2020-07-27 to 2025-12-31** (1,362 to 1,367 bars per symbol); DIS and SPY have one isolated earlier bar each.

## Trials and studies
A **study** is one search on one symbol; a **trial** is one evaluated (fast, slow) pair on one window with a phase (`train`, `test`, `holdout`). `grid_search` evaluates every pair with fast < slow (at most 400) and `record_trials` appends one row per pair. Re-running a search adds trials: attempts count. Nothing deletes weak trials. Ranking: by the metric (Sharpe by default), undefined values last, ties by smaller slow then smaller fast.

## Splits, embargo, warm-up, holdout
Time order only. `split_bars(n, train, embargo, test, holdout)` gives half-open index ranges: train `[0, train)`, test from `train + embargo`, holdout at the end preceded by an embargo. `evaluate_split` selects on the training slice only (so test and holdout bars cannot influence it; tested by scrambling them), then runs the chosen pair on the test block with `slow` earlier bars as warm-up, starting flat; the scored curve begins with a baseline row of initial cash. Minimums: 300 train and 60 test bars. `final_holdout_evaluation` may be called once per study (second call: `HoldoutAlreadyUsedError`, HTTP 409). Decay = in-sample Sharpe minus out-of-sample Sharpe of the selected pair.

## Walk-forward
Rolling: fold k trains on `[k*test, k*test + train)`, tests the next `test` bars after the embargo; step = test length. 1,366 bars with train 500, test 100, embargo 5 give 8 folds (tests start at index 505, last test ends at index 1,304). Each fold selects on its own training window, starts flat in cash, and the test blocks are chained by growth factor (the embargo bars between a fold's train and test are not in the curve; they are assumed cash). The report lists parameter changes between folds.

## Probabilistic and deflated Sharpe
Per-period Sharpe `SR = mean/std` (ddof 1), `T` returns, skewness `g3 = m3/m2^1.5`, kurtosis `g4 = m4/m2^2` (3 for normal):

`PSR(SR*) = Phi( (SR - SR*) sqrt(T-1) / sqrt(1 - g3 SR + (g4-1)/4 SR^2) )`

`SR0 = sqrt(V) ((1-gamma) Phi^-1(1 - 1/N) + gamma Phi^-1(1 - 1/(N e)))` (0 for N = 1), `DSR = PSR(SR0)`. `V` is the variance of the trial Sharpe estimates, `N` the number of trials in the registry. Checks in the tests: analytic values 0.977 (T = 1000) and 0.840 (T = 250) for an annualised Sharpe of 1; on 200 no-skill studies of 50 strategies, 92.5% of the best strategies had PSR(0) > 0.95 but 0% had DSR > 0.95 (seeded, thresholds written beforehand). The meter treats trials as independent and cannot count searches made outside the registry: a warning light, not a verdict.

## Universe scan and best-of-N
`universe_scan` runs one spec on every symbol with at least `min_bars` bars. `best_of_n_control` compares the real best Sharpe with the maximum over N random-walk symbols (GBM with each symbol's own mean and volatility) in R replicates. The controls **keep each symbol's historical drift**, which in this window is strongly positive; with independent returns a crossover rule is invested about half the time and still collects part of that drift. On the loaded data the real best-of-53 (Sharpe 1.08) lay below every simulated maximum (see the worked example); read this as "the rule did not beat what a drift-only random world gives", not as proof of anything else. A demeaned control and a correlation-adjusted N are deferred. The universe is today's survivors on the free IEX feed.

## Regimes
By calendar year, or by trailing 20-day volatility terciles (label from the previous close; thresholds from the full sample, descriptive). Groups reconcile: the product of group growth factors equals the run's total growth (checked, else error). Cells with few trades (often 2 to 5) are anecdotes.

## Sprint 05 additions
- **Longer data.** With Tiingo the same questions can be asked on 21 years (`docs/02-domain/long-window-example.md`): more folds, more trades per block, regimes that include 2008 and 2020. The page repeats the questions in three set-ups (Alpaca 5.4 y; Tiingo 5.4 y; Tiingo 21 y total return) so the effect of the source and the effect of the window can be told apart. N (the trial count) does not shrink with more data.
- **`source` and `price_basis`** are options of every validation request; when not the defaults they are stored in each trial's `params`, so the overfitting meter reloads the same bars.
- **VIX regimes.** `by = vix` labels each day by the previous trading day's `VIXCLS` close in fixed buckets chosen in advance (`low` < 15, `medium` 15 to 25, `high` >= 25, `unknown` before the first observation). Unlike the volatility terciles, the thresholds do not depend on the sample. The group growth factors multiply to the run's total growth (checked on real SPY and AAPL). `risk_free: true` on `/validation/regimes` adds the run's plain and excess Sharpe. This product uses the FRED API but is not endorsed or certified by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.

## Known limitations
Short window (about 5.4 years, mostly one market cycle); few trades per block; correlated parameter pairs and symbols (effective N below N); no dividends in returns; risk-free rate 0; flat bps costs; one strategy family; survivor universe. Nothing here is investment advice.
