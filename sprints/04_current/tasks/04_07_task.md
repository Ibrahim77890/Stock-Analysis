# Task 04_07: Regime breakdown by year and by volatility

**Sprint:** 04     **Depends on:** 04_01     **Size:** M
**Concepts to read first:** [04_06](../concepts/04_06_regimes-and-non-stationarity.md)

## Goal
Any backtest result can be split by calendar year or by trailing-volatility tercile, with each group's growth factor, exposure and trade count, and the groups reconcile to the whole run.

## Context
Blueprint 23.2 "Regime breakdown" and 23.1 (distrust results). It uses only the Sprint 03 engine output, so it can run in parallel to 04_02 to 04_06.

## What to do
1. Implement `regime_labels(bars, by)` returning one label per bar: `year` (the bar's calendar year) or `vol` (low, medium, high from the trailing 20-day annualised volatility of the asset's close, shifted by one day, thresholds at the 33rd and 67th percentile of the available values; the first labels without volatility are `warmup`).
2. Implement `regime_breakdown(result, bars, by)`: for each label return days, share of days with a position, growth factor (product of `1 + daily equity return`), return, and the number of trades whose entry date falls in the label.
3. Verify reconciliation inside the function: the product of group growth factors equals the run's total growth within 1e-9, otherwise raise `AssertionError`-style `ValueError`.
4. State in the output that tercile thresholds use the full sample (descriptive, not causal).
5. Document in `docs/02-domain/validation.md` with the SPY and AAPL SMA 20/50 tables.

## Where to do it
- Folder / file: `services/market-data/src/market_data/validation.py` (or `regimes.py`; record the choice), tests in `services/market-data/tests/test_regimes.py`.
- Technology: Python, Polars, existing `indicators.rolling_volatility`.
- Database schema/ownership: none (pure functions on frames).

## Inputs
- Sprint 03 `BacktestResult`; SPY and AAPL bars.

## Deliverables
- `regime_labels`, `regime_breakdown`, tests, docs tables.

## Acceptance criteria / tests
- [ ] Golden: a 6-bar hand-made equity curve split into two years gives the hand-computed growth factors and `1.20 * 0.95 = 1.14` style product.
- [ ] Reconciliation: product of group growth factors equals the total growth on real SPY and AAPL (1e-9) for both `year` and `vol`.
- [ ] No same-day leakage: changing today's close does not change today's label (the label uses the previous close's trailing volatility).
- [ ] Every bar gets exactly one label and day counts sum to the number of equity rows.
- [ ] Negative tests: unknown `by`, equity and bars with different dates, fewer than 25 bars for `vol` raise `ValueError`.
- [ ] Real run: record in the completion note which year/regime contributed most of SPY's return and how many trades each volatility group has.
- [ ] Standing invariants: causality of the labels, reconciliation.

## Out of scope
- Causal (expanding-window) terciles, trend regimes, market-wide regimes, hidden Markov models.

## Notes for the implementer
Do not choose the regime definition after looking at which makes the rule look best; the definitions here are fixed in advance.
