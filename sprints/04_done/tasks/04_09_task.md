## Context
Every sprint has a documentation task and a wrap-up.

## What to do
1. Finish `docs/02-domain/validation.md`: protocol summary (link to ADR 0012), formulas and estimators, the trial-counting rules, the holdout rule, limitations (short windows, correlated trials, descriptive terciles, no dividends, survivorship).
2.
