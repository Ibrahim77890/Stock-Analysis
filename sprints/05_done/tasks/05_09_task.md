## Goal
Sprint 04's validation story is rerun on the long Tiingo history with total-return prices and excess Sharpe, set next to the 5.4-year results, and the sprint is documented, reproduced on a fresh database and ready to close.

## Context
The audit showed 5.4 years of data and 1 to 5 trades per block. A twenty-year window is the main reason for this sprint.

## What to do
