# Task 09_06: Web: decision panel and My decisions journal with gentle review

**Sprint:** 09     **Depends on:** 09_04     **Size:** L
**Concepts to read first:** [09_03](../concepts/09_03_decision-journals-and-reflective-practice-for-beginners.md), [09_05](../concepts/09_05_accessible-tabs-toggles-and-secondary-chart-panels.md)

## Goal
While guessing, a learner can record a stance with a reason, and later see their decisions with what happened next and a few reflection questions, with no grade.

## Context
Blueprint MVP item 7. The journal is where the lessons about doubt become a habit.

## What to do
1. A "Practice decision" card on the episode screen: choose Long or Flat, type a reason (280 characters, counter), confirm; a client key prevents double submit; the result shows as "Waiting for the next day" until it exists.
2. A page `/journal` ("My decisions") listing decisions per episode: day, stance, reason, result in words, a count line ("6 decisions; this is too few to say much") and no win rate without the count.
3. Review prompts (copy): what did I expect, what happened, what did I miss; shown only after the episode is revealed.
4. Navigation link and a tour note.
5. Tests: double click sends one request; reason limit; result hidden until available; no score, rank or percentage-of-wins text; unrevealed episodes show no identity; errors plain.

## Where to do it
`apps/web/src/journal/`, `src/copy/journal.ts`, `App.tsx`, `test/journal.test.tsx`.

## Inputs
Gateway routes from 09_04.

## Deliverables
Decision card, journal page, copy, tests.

## Acceptance criteria / tests
- [ ] Keyboard-only use works; the reason field has a visible label and a character counter announced politely.
- [ ] No text says score, rank, points, level, win rate without a count, or "you should".
- [ ] The journal's counts equal the gateway's numbers (reconciliation).
- [ ] Copy, contrast and all earlier web tests pass.

## Out of scope
Charts of results, export, sharing.

## Notes for the implementer
Never shame a loss; show the reason the learner wrote and the count.
