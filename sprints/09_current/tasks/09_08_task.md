# Task 09_08: Cross-layer checks: causality, replay boundary, journal reconciliation, walks

**Sprint:** 09     **Depends on:** 09_02 to 09_07     **Size:** L
**Concepts to read first:** [09_02](../concepts/09_02_paper-trading-without-future-leakage-recording-a-decision-before-the-outcome.md), [09_06](../concepts/09_06_append-only-records-and-reconciling-a-ledger.md), [09_05](../concepts/09_05_accessible-tabs-toggles-and-secondary-chart-panels.md)

## Goal
On the real stack and database, show that indicators and decisions never leak the future or the identity of an open episode, that the journal reconciles, and that the new screens work from the keyboard.

## Context
Sprints 06 to 08 proved leak, isolation, contract and walk properties for their features; this does the same for indicators and decisions.

## What to do
1. **Leak fuzz (real stack):** real and synthetic mixed episodes; advance randomly; read indicators and decisions at every step; scan every reply and header for dates, symbols, kinds and values for days beyond the revealed day; planted leak detected.
2. **Causality at the HTTP layer:** the indicator values at day t are identical whether read at day t or after revealing the whole episode.
3. **Idempotency and concurrency:** 20 identical decision posts (same client key) leave one row; 30 concurrent decisions with a limit of 30 and another 10 refused.
4. **Reconciliation:** recompute every result in a script from `research.paper_decision`; totals equal the API's.
5. **Isolation fuzz** of 200 attempts; contracts with Ajv and a renamed-field negative.
6. **Browser walks:** extend `walk.mjs` with `--journal` (toggle an indicator, record a decision, advance, see the result) and `--journal --keyboard` (0 clicks); accessibility-tree check on the new screens.
7. **Latency** of the indicator and decision routes into `docs/03-benchmarks/benchmark-06-indicators-and-journal.md`.
8. Count and list test data left in the real database.

## Where to do it
`apps/api/test/journal-e2e.spec.ts`, `apps/web/scripts/walk.mjs`, `services/market-data/scripts/reports/`, `docs/03-benchmarks/`.

## Inputs
The running stack.

## Deliverables
Specs, walk modes, benchmark note, completion note.

## Acceptance criteria / tests
- [ ] Leak fuzz finds nothing; the planted leak is detected (negative).
- [ ] Indicator values are identical when read early and late.
- [ ] One row after 20 identical posts; exactly 30 of 40 concurrent decisions accepted.
- [ ] Recomputed results equal API totals.
- [ ] `--journal` and `--journal --keyboard` pass (0 clicks); an unnamed button fails the accessibility check (negative, then revert).
- [ ] Benchmark note has p50, p95, max and says what it does not show.

## Out of scope
Load testing, screen-reader testing (09_01).

## Notes for the implementer
A failed check is a finding: fix the cause in the lowest layer and keep a regression test.
