# What indicators and simple rules can and cannot tell you

**Used by tasks:** 09_07

## What it is
If prices already reflect available information, a pattern in past prices should not reliably predict the future after costs. The efficient-market idea is debated, but it is the right starting doubt for a beginner: a chart pattern that "looks like it works" has to survive costs, many tries and unseen data (the lessons of Sprints 06 to 08).

## Why it matters here
Lesson 7 connects indicators to those ideas without promising or denying anything: indicators describe; a rule built on one is a hypothesis; the Lab is where a hypothesis is tested honestly.

## Key ideas
- Description versus prediction.
- Every indicator rule is one more idea tried.
- Costs and the vault still apply.

## Common mistakes
Saying markets are "random" as a fact, or that an indicator "works".

## Check yourself
1. Is "the price is above its average" a forecast? 2. What would you need to test an indicator rule fairly?
